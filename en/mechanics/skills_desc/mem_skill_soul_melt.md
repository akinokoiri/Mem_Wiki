### Kills & Soul Recovery {#击杀与回魂}

When the player makes a kill, if the target has a soul and maximum Health **greater than 150**, restores [灵魂值] equal to **3%** of the target's maximum Health.

### Targets & Damage Sources {#目标与伤害来源}

- If `Wortox` cannot collect a soul from a creature, this effect cannot apply to that creature either.
- For Mangem's effects, all Clockworks are also treated as having no soul.
- <span class="heimu" title="Behind the scenes">Counterintuitively, lunar Bosses such as the **Celestial Champion** are not considered to have souls by the base game either. Does that mean the Celestial Champion is merely a puppet? Or is it just for so-called “game balance”?</span>
- This effect also **ignores distance**: as long as the damage source is the player, it can be checked, including some **damage-over-time effects**.
- Conversely, even if a `Spider` dies right beside you, the effect does not trigger when **you did not kill it** and its maximum `Health` is below 150.
