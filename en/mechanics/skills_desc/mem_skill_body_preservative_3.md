### Preserving Items & Food Holes {#物品与坑洞保鲜}

- The [腐烂速率] of carried items becomes **70%** of normal; applies to both the main inventory and backpacks.
- Preservation containers stack **multiplicatively** with the skill. For example, with a preserving backpack that gives a 50% spoilage rate: `50% × 70% = 35%`, which is the final spoilage rate.
- [藏食物] holes also multiply their existing Salt Box-level preservation by this factor; the effect is determined by the skill rank **when the hole is dug**.

### Corpse Decay Time {#尸体腐烂时间}

- Under normal surface conditions, [芒芒的尸体]'s [腐烂时间] increases to approximately **20 days** (about 3 days without the skill; environmental factors such as temperature and moisture affect the actual time). This is the duration, not 20 times the original duration.

### Interaction with Highly Flammable and Explosive {#与易燃易爆的联动}

This rank also increases [易燃易爆]'s meat-return chance per roll by **+5 percentage points**; all three ranks in the same branch count separately. See [易燃易爆] for the full corpse's six independent rolls, when the probability is recorded, and freshness.
