### Courage {#胆识}

Gain 10 Courage.
- When the **night-attack warning** triggers in darkness, Courage begins to accumulate at an increasing rate.
- Returning to the light resets the accumulation rate but preserves your Courage.
- Being hit by a **night attack** before reaching the target interrupts the current accumulation and multiplies current total progress by **0.05**, retaining **5%** and losing **95%** of existing Courage.
- Once you reach 10 points, **night attacks** no longer reduce Courage.
