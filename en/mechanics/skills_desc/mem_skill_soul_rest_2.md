### Conversion Rates & Crafting-Menu Exchanges {#转换率与制作栏兑换}

[休养死息] affects overhealing, out-of-combat Soul-to-Health conversion, and conversion of remaining Health to Soul on death.

| Rank | Conversion Rate | Crafting-Menu Cost | Crafting-Menu Output |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Not learned | 30% | 20 | 5.4 |
| Rank I | 50% | 20 | 9 |
| Rank II | 70% | 20 | 12.6 |

Crafting-menu exchanges work in both directions, with output equal to `20 × conversion rate × 90%`. See [灵魂值系统] for the conditions that enable passive healing and restrictions from [灵魂震荡].
