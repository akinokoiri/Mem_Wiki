### Revival & Teleportation {#复活与传送}

- Reviving at [芒芒的坟墓] preserves the grave and starts a **1-day revival cooldown**.
- Resting in [狐狸的凶宅] restores `Sanity` normally.
- Reviving from [芒芒的尸体] leaves a [归途之花], a **single-use** teleport anchor that withers after use. The cooldown for creating a new flower is **1 day**.

Ghost map teleportation is already a base ability and no longer requires this skill; immunity to [灵魂裂痕] is now provided by [魂墙(技能)].
