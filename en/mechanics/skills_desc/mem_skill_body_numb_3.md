### Sanity Effects {#理智值效果}

- For example, after learning this skill, a `Sanity aura` of <DST icon="sanity">60/60</DST> affects Mangem at only <DST icon="sanity">33/60</DST>.
- Affects continuous **Sanity auras and [理智值修正]**; both **positive recovery and negative drain** are reduced.
- Does not affect **instant changes** (*such as `Sanity` restored by food*).

### Damage-Taken & Attack Multipliers {#承伤和攻击倍率效果}

- For example, before learning the skill, the damage-taken and attack multipliers in [怨灵] at 50% `Sanity` are: 1 + (0.5 × 0.5) = 1.25 times.
- After learning it, they become: 1 + (0.95 × 0.5) = 1.475 times.

### Interaction with Highly Flammable and Explosive {#与易燃易爆的联动}

This rank also increases [易燃易爆]'s meat-return chance per roll by **+5 percentage points**; all three ranks in the same branch count separately. See [易燃易爆] for the full corpse's six independent rolls, when the probability is recorded, and freshness.
