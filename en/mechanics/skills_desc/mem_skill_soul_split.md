### Controls & Orbiting Ghost Fire {#操作与环绕鬼火}

- To change the default keybinding, scroll to the bottom of `ESC → Options → Controls`.
- [鬼火] continuously searches for nearby enemies, expanding and contracting its orbit to try to collide with targets.
- Minimum orbit radius: 2.5 units (slightly less than half a turf tile); maximum: 14 units (about half a screen).
- Requires at least **5 [灵魂值]** to activate; activation costs **1**, followed by a default drain of **0.5 per second**. It no longer deducts 40 Soul/maximum Soul, and there is no refund on deactivation.
- By default, each [鬼火] collision hit costs an additional **0.5 [灵魂值]**. Ongoing and collision costs can be adjusted separately in [Mod Settings](/en/mechanics/settings#灵魂与刻印), and both can be set to 0.
- During [分头行动]/[意识转移], the head and body each have **4 [鬼火]** orbiting them; damage and Soul cost per collision are each halved, while the per-second drain does not increase.
