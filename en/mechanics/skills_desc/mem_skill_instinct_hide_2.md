### Hunting & Concealment {#捕猎与隐蔽}



- Cumulative hunting success chance **+20 percentage points**; cumulative movement speed in Hunting Stance **+40%**.
- Attempting a capture from behind the target in Hunting Stance grants an additional **+30 percentage points** to success chance (the final probability is limited to 0–100%).
- Before an ordinary enemy has targeted you, its chance to ignore you when acquiring a target increases to **60%**; this is the enemy's targeting probability, not your hunting success chance.
- After jumping in Hunting Stance, automatically attempts to capture the nearest eligible target around you.


### Small Animals in Beast Form {#兽化下的小动物}

- Only works in [兽化]; creatures such as `Rabbits` and `Birds` still flee from you in normal form or [怨灵].
