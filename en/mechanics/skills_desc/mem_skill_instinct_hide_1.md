### Hunting & Concealment {#捕猎与隐蔽}



- Cumulative hunting success chance **+10 percentage points**; cumulative movement speed in Hunting Stance **+20%**.
- Attempting a capture from behind the target in Hunting Stance grants an additional **+30 percentage points** to success chance (the final probability is limited to 0–100%).
- Before an ordinary enemy has targeted you, its chance to ignore you when acquiring a target increases to **50%**; this is the enemy's targeting probability, not your hunting success chance.


### Neutral Creatures in Beast Form {#兽化下的中立生物}

- Only works in [兽化]; creatures such as `Pigmen` still attack you in [怨灵].
