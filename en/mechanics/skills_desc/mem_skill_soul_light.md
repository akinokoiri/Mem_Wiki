### Dodging Night Attacks {#夜袭闪避}

Once learned and active, **completely dodges Charlie's night attacks**. With [技能觉醒] enabled, you must first complete the Courage challenge below; with awakening disabled, the effect activates as soon as you meet the prerequisites and learn the skill.

- Each successful night-attack dodge grants **1 stack of [闪耀刻印]** and triggers the dodge visual effect.
- Requires no existing marks and costs no [灵魂值]; while you have marks, an attached Ghost Fire provides light.
- After learning [Soul Entity Mastery III](/en/mechanics/skilltree#mem_skill_soul_fire_3), you can recover [灵魂值] when the marks expire naturally or overflow their stack limit; Soul is not restored immediately on dodging.
- This effect applies only to Charlie's night attacks and does not guarantee dodging other attacks.

### Courage & Awakening {#胆识与觉醒}

Requires [融魂术]. With [技能觉醒] enabled, accumulate **10 Courage**: after hearing the night-attack warning in darkness, **return to light before being hit** to bank progress. The longer you stay after the warning, the more progress you gain on escaping successfully. Being hit by a night attack before reaching the target multiplies current total progress by **0.05**, retaining **5%** and losing **95%**. Once the target is met, this no longer reduces progress.

Normal awakening mode lets you accumulate Courage in advance; Strict mode only starts recording progress after the skill is learned. With awakening disabled, the challenge is not required.

### Taking Hits & Dodging {#受击与闪避}

- Taking a hit randomly grants **1–5 stacks of [闪耀刻印]**; while you have any stacks, a glowing Ghost Fire is attached to you.
- When [闪耀刻印] successfully dodges an attack, the stacks consumed become [魂魄刻印] on the attacker.
- These effects also apply to clones created by [分头行动]/[意识转移].
