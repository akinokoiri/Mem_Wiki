### Two Challenges: Complete Either One {#两项挑战-完成任一项}

With [技能觉醒] enabled, craft **66 different types of equippable gear**, or kill **6 qualifying creature types**, to activate a learned [死体精通]. The counts for the two routes are separate and cannot be combined or converted into each other.

### Which Equipment Counts {#哪些装备计入}

Equippable items for the hand, head, body, back, and neck slots all count. Progress is deduplicated by **item type**; crafting multiple copies of the same equipment counts as only one type.

### Which Creatures Count {#哪些生物计入}

The following categories are included:

- **Lunar-mutated creatures**: For example, Brightbeaks, Armored Beargers, Crystal-Crested Buzzards, Horror Hounds, and Misshapen Birds.
- **The mod's friendly followers**: Includes [友善的芒伊月], [友善的荒尹沐], and their friendly shadow bodies and shadow servants.
- **Creatures revived after shadow parasitism**: For example, Spiders or Bunnymen killed by masked creatures and revived wearing a Void Masque.

Counts are deduplicated by creature type and qualifying category; repeated kills of the same type in the same category do not count again. Eligibility is determined by the categories above, not simply whether a creature appears to “revive.”
