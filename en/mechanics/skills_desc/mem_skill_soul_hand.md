### Unarmed Soul Recovery {#空手回魂}

An unarmed hit on an enemy with a soul restores [灵魂值] equal to **3%** of the damage dealt.

### Units without Souls {#无灵魂-的单位}

- If `Wortox` cannot collect a soul from a creature, this effect cannot apply to that creature either.
- For Mangem's effects, all Clockworks are also treated as having no soul.
