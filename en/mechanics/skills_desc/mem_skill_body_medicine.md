### Allied Bounce Targets {#友军跳板}

- [鬼火] only attempts to bounce toward allies when there are **no other attackable hostile targets** nearby.
- Hitting an allied bounce target deals no damage, provokes no aggro, and does not detonate [魂魄刻印], so it also grants no [闪耀刻印] from detonation.
- Automatic bounces can search for **all players**, creatures marked as followers or companions, domesticated or hitched creatures, mod [友善随从] and their derivatives, `Abigail`, and `Glommer`; **they do not need to belong to you**, and must be alive.
- Manual cast target selection, initial aggro, and Ghost Fire hit resolution share the same ally check, which also recognizes allies confirmed by the game and Beefalo with some domestication. Targets qualifying only through these two conditions, without the category markers above, are not necessarily found by automatic bounce searches.
- This can help prevent Ghost Fire from disappearing for lack of a bounce target when only one hostile creature is present, despite still having bounces remaining.

### Mangelune's Weakness Duration {#芒伊月的虚弱时间}

The weakness period after a [友善的芒伊月] attacks in [近战模式] is reduced by **2 seconds**.

### Faction Coordination {#阵营协调}

- Your followers will not fight one another because of **opposing factions**.
- For example, a `Pigman` following you will not attack a `Merm` or `Spider` that is also following you.
- All your followers also gain **shared aggro**. When any follower is attacked, all followers within a certain range acquire the attacker as a target and retaliate.

### Planar Coordination {#位面协调}

After eating the corresponding material, the buff lasts **8 minutes**.

- Eating `Dreadstone` grants the <DSTIcon icon="shadowaligned"/>**Shadow Faction** buff; eating `Pure Brilliance` grants the <DSTIcon icon="moonaligned"/>**Lunar Faction** buff.
- +20% damage against the opposing faction and -20% damage taken from the same faction.
- Applies to the player and **all followers**, including **followers of followers**.
- Both existing followers and those that join later receive the buff.
- A follower with its own **faction alignment** cannot receive the **opposing faction's** buff.
- For example, [友善的荒尹沐], aligned with the <DSTIcon icon="shadowaligned"/>**Shadow Faction**, cannot receive the buff granting <u>**-20% damage taken from the <DSTIcon icon="moonaligned"/>Lunar Faction and +20% damage against the <DSTIcon icon="shadowaligned"/>Shadow Faction**</u>.
