### Losing Soul in a Short Time {#短时间损失灵魂}

- Lose 100% of your [灵魂值] within 5 seconds.
- Maximum [灵魂值] lost for other reasons can **reduce** how much [灵魂值] must be spent.
- For example, suppose a 100-point [灵魂值] pool loses 25% of its maximum [灵魂值] to [灵魂裂痕]/[灵魂震荡].
- You then only need to lose 100 - (100 × 25%) = 75 [灵魂值] within 5 seconds to meet the condition.
