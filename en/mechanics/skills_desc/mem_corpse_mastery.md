### Tactical Gear {#战术装备}

Once the skill is active, specific equipment can change how resurrected followers fight:

| Follower | Tactical Gear | Main Changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [友善的芒伊月] | Enlightened Crown | Ranged attacks become three overlapping Gestalt Fields; melee uses only leaping slashes, with improved performance during infusion and weakness |
| [友善的荒尹沐] | Sage's Masque | The body and shadows switch from a work crew to a combat squad, the summon limit increases by +2, Shadow Overload no longer increases, and effects such as a masked-creature disguise are granted |

The follower's **current follow target** must have learned this skill. See [Mangelune's Tactical Gear](/en/mechanics/enemies.html#-2) and [Mangmire's Tactical Gear](/en/mechanics/enemies.html#-3) for detailed moves, equipment durability, and unequipping rules.

### Corpse Embalming Core {#尸体防腐核心}

Learning and activating this skill unlocks [尸体防腐核心], crafted from **1 Blue Gem + 1 Salt + 1 Infused Moon Shard**. Hold the core on the cursor and use it on an eligible target to upgrade it.

| Upgrade Target | Main Effect |
| --- | --- |
| [芒芒的坟墓] | Never spoils; revival by haunting grants an additional +25% [最低三维比例] |
| [芒芒的尸体] | Improves preservation and prevents [位面寄生]; increases the inherited Hunger and Sanity proportions on revival and grants an additional +25% minimum three-stat ratio |
| Mangem | Drying rate +50% |
| [友善随从] | Increases received healing |

Corpse preservation depends on **world moisture**: decay is only slightly delayed during spring rain, while dry conditions can extend it greatly or even preserve the corpse indefinitely. See [尸体防腐核心] for the full rules.

### Salt & Follower Healing {#盐与随从治疗}

Giving Salt or Brightshade Husks to [友善的芒伊月], or Salt or Dreadstone to [友善的荒尹沐], normally restores **20% of maximum Health**; the core upgrade raises this to **40%**.

The core also improves ongoing healing: Mangelune's infused Brightshade Sword healing rises from **3** to **4.5 Health** every 5 seconds; Mangmire's mask healing rises from **2** to **3 Health** every 5 seconds. The latter consumes mask durability.

See [Mangelune's Healing & Repairs](/en/mechanics/enemies.html#yue-pet-care) and [Mangmire's Healing & Repairs](/en/mechanics/enemies.html#shadow-pet-care) for the specific conditions.

### Upgrade Inheritance {#升级的继承}

The corpse left by a player on death inherits the embalming upgrade; a player who revives by haunting an upgraded corpse inherits it too. The upgrade is also preserved when corpses and [友善随从] transform into one another, and when followers become dormant or revive.
