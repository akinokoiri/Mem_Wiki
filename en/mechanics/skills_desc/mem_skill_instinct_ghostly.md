### Detection Range & Shadow Creatures {#察觉范围与影怪}

- Reduces the **maximum range** at which **enemies can notice you** to 8 units.
- During [分头行动], this **maximum range** becomes 5 units.
- [捕猎姿态] applies a further ×60% multiplier.
- Reduces the maximum range of your [嘲讽] to 8 units.
- Separating during [分头行动]/[意识转移] makes nearby **terror-type and nightmare-type shadow creatures** currently targeting you lose aggro; reattaching also frightens qualifying nearby shadow creatures. These two actions have different check ranges and target conditions.
- **`Sanity` recovery** from killing **terror-type shadow creatures** (the shadows that appear at low Sanity) increases by +50%.
- For example, killing a `Terrorbeak` normally gives <DST icon="sanity">+33</DST>; after learning the skill, killing a `Terrorbeak` gives <DST icon="sanity">+49.5</DST>.
