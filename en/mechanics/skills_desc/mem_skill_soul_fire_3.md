### Mark Resolution & Recovery {#刻印结算与恢复}

When [闪耀刻印] **expires naturally** or **overflows its stack limit**, it refunds the [灵魂值] spent applying [魂魄刻印], based on the number of stacks resolved.

- Resolving **5 / 10 stacks** also restores **5% / 10%** of maximum [灵魂值].
- Targets without a [灵魂值系统] instead recover a small amount of `Health`.
- A clone's recovery is passed to the player; when the clone disappears, its remaining marks also transfer to the player.

See [闪耀刻印] for full stack, duration, and dodge rules.

### Interaction with Highly Flammable and Explosive {#与易燃易爆的联动}

This rank also increases [易燃易爆]'s meat-return chance per roll by **+5 percentage points**; all three ranks in the same branch count separately. See [易燃易爆] for the full corpse's six independent rolls, when the probability is recorded, and freshness.
