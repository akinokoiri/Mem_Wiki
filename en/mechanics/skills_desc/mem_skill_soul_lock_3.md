### Peak Soul Pool {#灵魂池峰值}

With [技能觉醒] enabled, your [灵魂池] must **reach at least 150 points at a single moment** to activate a learned [四象离魂].

This is the peak amount stored in the pool, not a cumulative total of 150 gained across the world. Build the Soul Pool through overhealing; see [灵魂池] for storage and consumption rules.

Learning this skill requires any two of [融魂术], [灵魂实体专精三级], and [休养死息二级].
