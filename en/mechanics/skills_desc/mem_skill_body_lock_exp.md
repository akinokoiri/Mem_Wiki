### Awakening Challenge {#觉醒挑战}

With [技能觉醒] enabled, you must **die from an explosion 1 time** to activate a learned [易燃易爆].

Taking explosion damage without dying does not fulfill this challenge. Learning the skill still requires both [野兽体质] and [幽灵体质]; challenge progress and skill-point prerequisites are checked separately.

Enter `/mem 易燃易爆` in the game to check awakening progress.
