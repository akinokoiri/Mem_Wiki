### A Single Soul Shock {#单次灵魂震荡}

With [技能觉醒] enabled, you must experience **one [灵魂震荡] of at least 126% magnitude** to activate a learned [魂墙(技能)].

This checks the magnitude of one shock; the accumulated amount of several smaller shocks does not satisfy the challenge. See [灵魂震荡] for triggers and magnitude calculations.

Learning this skill requires [休养死息二级].
