### Ghost Fire Bounces & Marks {#鬼火弹射与刻印}

- [鬼火] fired by you can **bounce 10 additional times** after its initial hit.
- Each bounce increases the next flight's speed and targeting range by **+10%** of the initial values: **×1.1, ×1.2, ×1.3…**.
- After your [鬼火] detonates [魂魄刻印], you gain an equal number of [闪耀刻印] stacks for **10 seconds**.

[闪耀刻印] maintaining [电锯轰鸣] stacks is already a base effect and does not require this skill.

### Interaction with Highly Flammable and Explosive {#与易燃易爆的联动}

This rank also increases [易燃易爆]'s meat-return chance per roll by **+5 percentage points**; all three ranks in the same branch count separately. See [易燃易爆] for the full corpse's six independent rolls, when the probability is recorded, and freshness.
