### Skill Effects {#技能效果}

- While active, grants immunity only to [灵魂裂痕] caused by death and resurrection. Crafting [芒芒的尸体] still reduces maximum [灵魂值]. It also grants **+100%** [灵魂震荡] recovery speed; it no longer grants shock immunity.
- Grants immunity to [暗影观察者] created by [怨灵]/[意识转移].
- Unlocks [魂墙(物品)], costing **1 [灵魂值]** per segment.
