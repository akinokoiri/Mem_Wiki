### Ghost Fire from Both Follower Types {#两类随从的鬼火}

- [鬼火] fired by a [友善的荒尹沐] following you gains **+5** bounces, increasing from **5 to 10** (not including the initial hit).
- Each [鬼火] fired by a [友善的芒伊月] following you deals **+67%** damage.

[闪耀刻印]'s dodge effect is already a base effect and does not require this skill: each stack gives a **5% dodge chance**, up to **50%**; a successful dodge halves the stacks.

### Interaction with Highly Flammable and Explosive {#与易燃易爆的联动}

This rank also increases [易燃易爆]'s meat-return chance per roll by **+5 percentage points**; all three ranks in the same branch count separately. See [易燃易爆] for the full corpse's six independent rolls, when the probability is recorded, and freshness.
