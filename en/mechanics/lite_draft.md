# Quick Start {#极速上手}

This guide is for players who already know Don't Starve and are trying Mangem for the first time. You can still explore, build a base, and fight bosses at your own pace. Below are the new options she brings, and the habits most worth adjusting early on.

## What Changes from Vanilla Characters? {#playstyle}

**Hunt while traveling and try a new rhythm for the early game.** Entering [兽化] increases movement speed by 35%, lets you eat raw meat, jump over obstacles, and strike enemies in rapid combos. With [野兽体质], you can also chop trees and mine unarmed. By linking exploration, hunting, and resupply, you can try sustaining your surface exploration through combat. However, [兽化] drains Hunger faster, prevents hand equipment use, and costs stats to enter and leave. Plan your pace around food reserves and your route.

**Leave your body behind and scout the road ahead.** [灵魂出窍] lets you voluntarily become a ghost, explore unknown areas at higher speed, then teleport back to your [尸体] and resurrect. It adds another option when finding the Lunar Island or scouting branches of the Ruins: establish your destination and route first, then travel there alive to gather resources.

**Challenge exclusive bosses, then put their moves to work for you.** Defeat the corresponding boss and use its drops to resurrect a matching [友善随从], which you can command to support combat or work. Turn the lasers and shadows that once chased you against your enemies, and give them a taste of their own medicine.

::: tip Suggested settings for a first playthrough
Start with the **Normal** preset: it eases some transformation penalties, disables low-Sanity [嘲讽], and keeps [技能觉醒] and [潜伏恐惧]. To learn the abilities with less extra pressure, choose **Casual** instead. It also disables [技能觉醒] and [潜伏恐惧], and improves followers' survivability and endurance.

The values below use the **Default configuration** as examples. “Normal” and “Default” are different presets. See [Mod Settings](/en/mechanics/settings#难度预设) for the exact differences.
:::

## Starting Out: Learn Beast Form {#starting-out}

Mangem's base caps are **75 Health, 150 Hunger, 200 Sanity, and 150 Soul**. She starts with [封印项圈] Lv1. Keep the collar on while learning to switch between human form and [兽化]; removing it also activates [怨灵], which you can leave for later.

### Try Beast Form Once {#先试一次兽化}

<div class="archive-illustrated">

<MediaCard position="inline" src="/videos/combat-attack-rhythm.webm" caption="Different attack rhythms in Beast Form" width="280px" :manual="true" :intrinsic-width="660" :intrinsic-height="450" />

<div class="archive-copy">

Press <kbd>V</kbd> to toggle [兽化], run a short distance, then press <kbd>R</kbd> to [跳跃] over nearby obstacles. With default controls, you jump in your movement direction while moving and toward the cursor while stationary. The distance you can cross is limited.

[兽化] supports unarmed combat and has innate damage reduction. Human form has only a **0.5** attack multiplier, so learning to kite ordinary early enemies such as Spiders in [兽化] with [封印项圈] equipped is a good start. Faster combos change the attack rhythm too. Against a Tallbird, for example, suitable spacing and execution may let you go from “hit once or twice, then dodge” to “hit four or five times, then dodge.” The actual number still depends on attack speed and positioning.

**[兽化] cannot use hand equipment, and worn equipment does not provide ordinary defensive effects.** Although it has **65%** innate damage reduction, its low maximum Health still makes standing your ground and trading hits risky.

Entering [兽化] costs [饱食度], and leaving costs [理智值], each calculated as **20%** of its corresponding maximum by default. Grouping exploration, gathering, or combat into one period of [兽化] saves repeated transformation costs. Once back at your stopping point, prepare the next trip's tools, plan your route, and recover together.

</div>
</div>

### Food: Turn Hunting into Resupply {#食物-让打猎接上补给}

Natural Hunger drain in [兽化] is **2 times** normal. As you explore, look for renewable food sources along the way and use your mobility to “borrow” some meat from passing Frogs or Pigs. After learning [Hiding Instinct II](/en/mechanics/skilltree#mem_skill_instinct_hide_2), you can also use [捕猎姿态] to catch small animals and pounce on birds. Monster Meat from Spiders can be eaten directly in [兽化] as a traveling supply.

[兽化] removes food's direct negative effects on the three basic stats, but food can still separately trigger [食物中毒], causing an ongoing loss of one stat and a slowdown for a short time. Mushrooms have a higher poisoning chance. Immunity to the food's original penalties and poisoning are separate mechanics; eating raw food is not entirely free of consequences.

For surplus food, **hold it on the cursor and right-click the ground** to [藏食物], gaining Salt Box-level preservation. Holes last only **1 day** by default, or **2 days** with [野兽体质], and drop their contents when they expire. They suit supplies you will return to retrieve soon.

When you are only crafting or organizing items at home, or temporarily lack enough food, leave [兽化] to restore normal natural Hunger drain.

### Lighting: Night Camp and a Test of Courage {#照明-夜晚扎营和试胆大会}

<div class="archive-illustrated">

<MediaCard position="inline" src="/videos/soul-light-night-dodge.webm" caption="Light of the Otherworld, Soulfire Orbit, and dodging night attacks" width="280px" :manual="true" :intrinsic-width="640" :intrinsic-height="600" />

<div class="archive-copy">

**[兽化] cannot hold a Torch or a Lantern.**

Early on, prepare a Campfire and fuel before dark. Use the night to cook meat hunted during the day, recover, and craft the tools and equipment you will need next. Unlocking recipes also restores [理智值]. If basic materials lie within the light, gather those too.

Take care when changing headgear: **removing [封印项圈] activates [怨灵]**. Although [封印项圈] has a slot for head equipment, items in that slot provide no stats during [兽化] if they do not meet Beast Form's wearable-equipment rules. Even in normal form, putting a Miner Hat or Moggles in the slot does not inherit extra effects such as light emission or night vision.

**If you are willing to test yourself against Charlie, [彼世的光芒] can be an early skill goal.** Learn its prerequisite [融魂术] first, then unlock it. With [技能觉醒] enabled, you must also accumulate **10 [Courage](/en/mechanics/skilltree#mem_skill_soul_light)** before it takes effect.

To build Courage, prepare a Torch in human form, enter darkness, listen for the night-attack warning, then equip the Torch and return to light before the attack lands. The longer you stay after the warning, the more Courage you gain upon safely returning to light. Being hit before reaching the goal multiplies current total progress by **0.05**, retaining **5%** and losing **95%**. Learn the warning and switching timing first; there is no need to push the limit immediately. The Casual preset disables awakening, so the skill works as soon as its prerequisite is met and it is learned.

Once active, the skill **completely dodges Charlie's night attacks**. Each successful dodge also grants **1 stack of [闪耀刻印]**, making the attached [鬼火] glow. If you also learn [Soul Entity Mastery III](/en/mechanics/skilltree#mem_skill_soul_fire_3), marks restore [灵魂值] when they expire naturally or exceed the stack limit. This effect is specific to Charlie's night attacks and does not remove other dangers in darkness.

Later, combine [彼世的光芒] with [四象离魂] for mobile lighting over a larger area. [四象离魂] costs **0.5 [灵魂值] per second** by default, and each [鬼火] collision hit costs another **0.5 points**. Both are adjustable in [Mod Settings](/en/mechanics/settings#灵魂与刻印).

### Sanity and Injury: Plan Each Step {#理智和受伤-规划好每一步}

Early ways to restore [理智值] are limited. Get comfortable with [兽化] first, and consider [怨灵] in the mid-to-late game.

Once you know shadow creatures' attack rhythms, use [兽化] mobility to handle small numbers of them, or add [怨灵] for more damage. [幽灵体质] also increases the [理智值] gained from killing terror-type shadow creatures. Killing [潜伏恐惧] restores a substantial amount of [理智值] too, but do not confuse it with ordinary shadow creatures. Constantly fighting shadows takes time away from gathering and exploration; early on, picking flowers along the way and making the most of every new recipe's Sanity restoration are often better choices.

[嘲讽], enabled by default, intermittently draws nearby creatures' aggro when **Sanity is below 50%**. At low Sanity, avoid dense groups such as Beefalo herds and the Pig King village. [兽化] itself is also treated as a monster by Pigs and similar creatures. The Normal preset disables [嘲讽], but you must still handle shadow creatures and [潜伏恐惧].

[顷刻炼化] restores Soul through unarmed hits on enemies that have souls; [融魂术] instead requires killing a target with a soul and maximum Health **greater than 150**. Once out-of-combat recovery conditions are met, the [灵魂值] gained can gradually convert into [生命值] to make up combat losses. Their triggers differ, so ordinary Spider fights mainly rely on the former.

When eating healing food at full Health, **a proportion of excess healing enters the [灵魂池], then gradually replenishes [灵魂值]**. Learning [休养死息] improves both excess-healing-to-Soul conversion and out-of-combat Soul-to-Health conversion.

**Consider these starting skill directions:**

- [顷刻炼化]: Restores Soul when unarmed attacks hit enemies with souls, helping [兽化] combat replenish some resources. It does not work on soulless units such as Clockworks.
- [休养死息]: Improves Health / Soul conversion efficiency, useful if you frequently need recovery.
- [逐渐麻木]: Reduces ongoing [理智值] changes, including monster auras and dusk / night drain. Positive recovery auras are weakened too, but instant restoration such as food is unaffected. It also increases both [怨灵]'s outgoing and incoming damage multipliers.
- [精准度“优化”]: Increases [兽化] gathering speed and unarmed damage, but slows crafting. With default values, **Level III** reduces the unarmed hits needed against a hundred-Health creature without additional damage reduction (such as an ordinary Spider) from four to three. It also unlocks the path to [野兽体质].
- [野兽体质]: Allows unarmed work in [兽化], faster [跳跃], and one extra day of storage for buried food.

None of these require an in-game [技能觉醒] challenge. See the [Skill Tree](/en/mechanics/skilltree) for full prerequisites, point costs, and other paths.

## Settling In: Easier Exploration and Switching {#settling-in}

### Scout a Route with One Out-of-Body Trip {#用一次出窍摸清路线}

When looking for islands or checking cave branches, first find **an out-of-combat spot that is easy to return to**, then press <kbd>C</kbd> for [灵魂出窍]. Items do not drop, ghosts can reveal the map, and movement speed increases by 200%.

When finished scouting, open the map, **right-click a valid [尸体] anchor to teleport back, then haunt the [尸体] to resurrect**. Ghost map teleportation needs no skill, although map teleportation restrictions still apply. Once you know the route, bring supplies and return for gathering or combat.

Out-of-combat projection creates a [灵魂裂痕] worth **5% of maximum Soul**, gradually repaired by the [灵魂池]. In-combat projection and other deaths cost more. You can try an out-of-body trip right at the start to find key locations such as Worm Holes, the Eye Bone, the Pig King village, and Junk Piles. If you know how to find islands, continue looking for the Lunar Island and Moon Quay (Monkey Island).

::: warning Watch where the corpse appears
A newly generated [尸体] can trigger [位面寄生] and become a boss. Environments such as the Ruins and Lunar Island affect the chance. For your first out-of-body trip, choose an ordinary, safe surface location. Read the [parasitism conditions](/en/mechanics/enemies#寄生概率因素) before trying it.
:::

<details class="archive-disclosure">
<summary>How do I get going again after an accidental death?</summary>

First check whether danger remains around the [尸体]. A ghost can haunt the [尸体] to resurrect, but resurrection after an ordinary death brings [灵魂震荡]: temporarily reduced stat caps, movement speed, attack multiplier, and work efficiency, plus impaired Soul recovery. Leaving the battlefield and recovering is safer than repeatedly reviving on the spot.

If [死亡回归] is available, press <kbd>X</kbd> as a ghost and resurrect within the next **10 seconds** to clear [灵魂震荡] and gain a brief, powerful escape buff. Its default cooldown is **7 days**, so do not treat it as unlimited, cost-free resurrection.

[灵魂震荡] recovers over time and can also be cleared by eating a Telltale Heart. [灵魂裂痕] requires separate repair through the [灵魂池]. See [Death and Resurrection](/en/mechanics/core#已死之人-死亡与复活) for the distinction.

</details>

### Upgrade the Collar Once You Have Materials {#有材料后-先升级项圈}

If you frequently switch between [兽化] and human form, make **Lv2 [封印项圈]** your first exclusive upgrade. Crafted at a Science Machine from the Lv1 collar, 3 Pig Skins, 2 Moon Rocks, 1 Stinger, and 1 Petal, it **halves the Sanity loss from leaving Beast Form**. Lv3 also reduces the Health loss when removing the collar. See [封印项圈] for recipes and defense values.

### After Building a Base {#建家后}

Haunt a Tent as a ghost to turn it into [狐狸的凶宅]. It unlocks exclusive crafting technology, provides a ghost teleportation anchor, and gives nearby Mangem a Sanity-restoring aura, making it useful at a frequently visited base.

**Sleeping in the Murder House drains Sanity by default**; only [落叶归根] changes this to normal recovery. Its Sanity effects on other characters differ as well. Discuss it before converting a Tent shared with other players.

## Ready to Fight! {#bosses-and-followers}

Both exclusive bosses come from [位面寄生] of a [尸体]; **you do not need to wait until after Celestial Champion** to encounter them. Once you have sustained lighting, supplies, and a familiar combat style, try deliberately creating the conditions for parasitism. Use [芒芒尸体的设计图] to check the chance at your location. Successful parasitism starts a cooldown.

### For a Portable Combat Follower: Mangelune {#想要便于携带的战斗随从-芒伊月}

<div class="archive-illustrated">

<MediaCard position="inline" src="/videos/tjjg_web.webm" caption="Mangelune's triple strike and Orbital Laser" width="320px" :manual="true" />

<div class="archive-copy">

The [芒伊月] fight includes [虚影侵蚀区], lasers, and a melee phase. Watch for the flashing markers, leave the fields that form around them, and look for attack openings; in the first phase, you can also direct [月能激光] into the boss itself. Later, [天基激光] appears alongside melee moves, so watch the positions of both. If its equipment is hard to handle (such as Brambleshade Armor), retreat first: **at the start of a new day, it receives new random equipment if it is out of combat**. See [Wartime Supplies](/en/mechanics/enemies#特殊机制-战时补给) for the rules.

Remember two things on your first encounter: **it can still counterattack while weakened, and the finishing laser must hit it when it is near death to complete the kill.** See the [Mangelune guide](/en/mechanics/enemies#def-芒伊月) for detailed counterplay.

With the matching drop, resurrect [友善的芒伊月]. It provides ranged support, enters timed melee mode when given certain weapons, and can call lasers through its bond item. You can temporarily [收回] it for easier travel. Melee has limited endurance, and damage requires repairs.

### For a Follower That Helps with Work: Mangmire {#想要能辅助工作的随从-荒尹沐}

The [荒尹沐] fight revolves around its main form, shadows, and separated head and body. Watch surrounding units and choose whether to clear interference or take an opening against the head. Focusing only on shadows may let the head heal and prolong the battle. See the [Mangmire guide](/en/mechanics/enemies#def-荒尹沐) for first-encounter preparation.

The corresponding [友善的荒尹沐] can organize shadows and bodies to chop trees and mine, or join combat on command. **Freshly summoned, it only follows by default; use its bond item to designate a work or combat location.** Maintaining shadows and bodies continuously drains the main follower's [生命值] through [暗影负载], and their injuries also feed back to it. Inspect [先驱意志], or use the radial menu's “Information” command, to check the follower's Health and status.

### How Do I Get a Follower After Winning? {#打赢之后-怎样获得随从}

Prepare a [芒芒的坟墓] to provide follower capacity, then give the corresponding boss's [被囚禁的虚影] or [被侵蚀的虚影] to another [尸体]. This resurrects the matching follower and grants its command bond item. The corpse's freshness affects the follower's current [生命值] at resurrection.

On your first outing with a follower, try a nearby fight or gathering trip to learn **commands, healing, and dormancy** before tackling other bosses. See [Obtaining and Maintaining Followers](/en/mechanics/enemies#follower-lifecycle) for materials, capacity, and commands.

## Once Comfortable, Try More {#more-to-try}

- **Low-Sanity Wraith combat.** After removing [封印项圈], lower [理智值] raises both outgoing and incoming damage multipliers. Hits can spend [灵魂值] or [理智值] to restore [生命值]; lethal damage can also consume these two resources to keep you alive. More overflow damage in a single hit yields a lower resource-conversion ratio, but surviving further hits still depends on remaining resources and recovery. See [Combat Continuation](/en/mechanics/core#专属技能-战斗续行). You can still die when resources run out, and continuing to attack can also drain Health fatally. Mounted combat is another option: [怨灵]'s attack bonus can transfer to your mount. [兽化] and [怨灵] can coexist, stacking their benefits and costs.
- **Chainsaw and marks.** [电锯惊魂] can be upgraded and supports combat built around consecutive attacks and mark detonations. After crafting it, learn its costs and repair methods, then gradually find upgrade materials. Treat it as a long-term equipment path.
- **Head Out.** Separate your head and body to try different controls and work methods. [分头行动] changes damage handling and controls; learn the relevant skills, then begin practicing in safe situations.

As you prepare for caves, the Ruins, or post-Celestial Champion challenges, add these abilities to your existing routes. To check a particular condition, consult [Character Mechanics](/en/mechanics/core), [Items and Food](/en/mechanics/items), [Enemies and Followers](/en/mechanics/enemies), and the [Skill Tree](/en/mechanics/skilltree).
